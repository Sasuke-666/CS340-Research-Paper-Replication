experiment execution, log analysis and report drafting. Measurements in this
report are taken from the preserved process logs. Synthetic unit-test fixtures
are excluded from experimental results. The recorded owner contribution is
selection confirmation, task specification and authorization; independent
student implementation or review is not implied. Each member must review and
understand the work and record their genuine contribution before final submission.
